ClipDuel · guide · Animate (Labs) · Video → talk/action

Make your own footage talk back.

Upload one video and mark up to three moments on its timeline. At each one, your subject says a line or does something. The app cuts to an AI-animated version of that moment, then back to your footage, and gives you one MP4.

Output one MP4 per video Moments up to 3 per video Cost ≈ $0.10 per second of animation Tab Animate (Labs) → Video → talk/action
Before you start
  • Animate (Labs) is a tab for the app's admins. It shows only when Animate is turned on for your app. Open it and choose Video → talk/action in the Mode menu.
  • Each animated moment is a paid call to a cloud AI video service. The editor shows the price before you pay, and running the exact same job again is free.
  • TTS voices and captions use the app's OpenAI connection. A recorded voice with captions off doesn't need it.

STEP 1 · UPLOAD

Upload your video

Pick the video you want to bring to life. The editor opens once it's uploaded.

  • Choose an MP4, MOV, WebM, or MKV file, then click Upload. Choosing the file doesn't upload it.
  • When it's in, the file's name, length, size, and frame rate show beside the button.
  • The editor works on one video at a time. Uploading another one clears the spans you placed on this one. Jobs you already submitted keep running.

Short clips work best. Each moment you animate is a few seconds long, and the rest of the video is your own footage.

STEP 2 · MARK A MOMENT

Find the moment, then add a span

A span is one AI moment. At its cut, the video switches to an animated version of that exact frame.

  • The Stage shows your video at the playhead. Click Play (or press Space) to watch, and pause where you want the cut.
  • Or click or drag along the timeline. The Source track is a filmstrip of your video.
  • Click + Add at playhead. You can add up to 3 spans, and the list beside the stage shows each one with its price.
  • To move a cut, drag the span's diamond, or type a time in Cut at (s). Cuts snap to a real frame.
  • Spans can't overlap. If two clash, a red line under the timeline says which one to fix.
  • Each span keeps its place in the list, in time order. Dragging a diamond stops at the spans on either side, but a typed cut doesn't. Type a time that passes another span's cut and a red line says the spans must be listed in cut order. To move a span past another one, remove it and add it again at the new spot.

The timeline has four tracks: Source (your video), Animation (one block per span), Voice (each spoken line's length), and Captions (on or off for each talk span).

STEP 3 · TALK

Make it say a line

Select a span and set its mode to talk. The subject lip-syncs the line, starting from the frame at the cut.

  • TTS voice: choose a voice, set its speed if you like, and type what it says (up to 200 characters, fewer below speed 1). Delivery is optional: tone, accent, or pacing.
  • Your recording: pick an audio file of up to 12 seconds instead.
  • A line runs 2 to 15 seconds on the app's current talk model. The span is as long as its line, so its edge doesn't drag.
  • Captions: pick a caption style, or No captions. They show only while it speaks.
  • Motion prompt (optional): how it moves while it talks. Tap a chip such as Turns to the camera or Camera holds still to add one.

Talk costs about $0.10 per second of the line, rounded up to whole seconds. A 4.6-second line costs about $0.50.

STEP 4 · ACTION

Or make it do something

Set the mode to action and describe what happens. The model starts from the frame at the cut.

  • Write up to 500 characters. Chips add common moves, such as Walks left, Sits down, or Slow push-in.
  • Length is 4–10 whole seconds. Type it, or drag the span's right edge on the timeline.
  • Seed (optional): leave it blank and the model picks. New seed picks a random one. Keep the seed of a take you like to get it again.
  • Action spans are silent.

Action costs about $0.09 per second at Final quality, and about $0.02 at Draft.

STEP 5 · REJOIN YOUR FOOTAGE

Choose how your footage carries on

Every span says what happens after the AI moment, under Then.

  • Resume where it cut adds the AI moment, and your footage carries on from the cut, so the video gets longer.
  • Skip ahead to… replaces your footage up to the time you set. Type it, or drag the span's right diamond.
  • End on the real frame (action spans that skip ahead): the AI clip lands on your footage's frame at that time, so the jump back is smooth. A preview of that frame shows below it.
  • Seam blend: tick Blend in or Blend out to dissolve the picture between your footage and the AI clip, over 0.1–2 seconds. Click the line to add points and shape the curve. Straight resets it.

A blend changes only the picture. It doesn't change what the subject does, and the sound still cuts where it did. A new curve re-renders for free. The panel tells you when a seam has little to do, for example when the clip already starts on your frame.

STEP 6 · CHECK THE PRICE

Check the price and quality

The editor prices the job as you build it, before anything is paid for.

  • The timeline's top line shows the whole job, for example ≈ $0.97 final · ≈ $0.61 draft · talk has no draft. ≈ means the line's length is an estimate until its voice is made.
  • Quality: Final, or Draft (480p, action only). A draft runs action spans cheaply at low resolution, so you can check the motion. Talk costs the same either way.
  • output ≈ shows how long the finished video will run.
  • If something isn't finished, a line under the timeline says what, such as Span 2 still needs an action prompt, and Animate stays greyed out.

The price covers the AI animation on a first run. The voice and the caption timing add a few cents. Running the exact same job again is free.

STEP 7 · OPTIONAL

Hear the lines before you pay

Hear the lines first makes each talk span's voice and caption timing, which costs pennies, then stops before any animation is paid for.

  • The job's card plays each line. If you like them, click Generate to make the animation.
  • If you don't, click Open in editor, change the words, and hear them again. Each try is its own card, and a wording you've already heard is free.
  • A try you don't generate just waits. It costs nothing more.
  • With no talk span there's nothing to hear, so the button is greyed out.
STEP 8 · THE RESULT

Animate, wait, then download

Click Animate (Animate draft at Draft quality). The job gets a card under the editor.

  • The card shows its status in the corner: queued, preparing, generating, splicing, then done or failed. One line per span ticks audio, words, and animated as each is ready. Action spans have no audio or words, so those tick right away.
  • The app animates one job at a time. A job you submit while another runs waits its turn.
  • When it's done, the card has a player and a Download link. The Stage gets Source frame and Result buttons, so you can compare.
  • Open in editor brings a job's spans back, so you can tune them and run it again. Anything unchanged is reused for free.
  • You can leave the page. If the app restarts, the job picks up where it stopped, and nothing already made is paid for again.

Animate videos aren't part of blind picks or the Schedule queue. Download yours and post it yourself.

If something is greyed out or fails

  • Animate is greyed out. Something isn't finished, such as a span with no line or prompt, or two spans that overlap. The lines under the timeline say what.
  • A red line says the spans must be listed in cut order. A typed cut passed another span's cut. Type a time between the spans on either side, or remove the span and add it again at the new spot.
  • + Add at playhead is greyed out. You already have 3 spans, or the playhead sits where a span can't start, such as inside another span or footage one of them skips.
  • TTS voices or captions are greyed out. They need the app's OpenAI connection. Use Your recording with No captions instead, or ask whoever runs your app.
  • A line is too short or too long. Lines run 2 to 15 seconds. Change the wording or the speed, or pick a different recording.
  • The Stage shows a still frame and no Play button. You reopened a job, or your browser can't play this file type. Scrubbing still shows each frame.
  • A job failed. The card says why. Fix it if it names your input, then submit again. Anything already generated is reused, so it isn't paid for twice.
