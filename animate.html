BEFORE YOU START · labs

Find the tab

Animate uses a paid AI video service, so it's off by default. When it's turned on for your app, a fourth tab, Animate (Labs), appears after Schedule.

  • If the video service isn't connected yet, the tab still shows but can't run: the three generated modes are greyed out in the Mode menu, and Video → talk/action shows a message saying what's missing.
  • A talk segment's TTS voice and captions use OpenAI. The other three modes don't.

The Mode menu starts on Video → talk/action the first time. After that, your browser remembers the mode you used last.

MODE 1 · IMAGE TO VIDEO

Animate one photo

Drop in a photo, say how it should move, and get one short clip that starts on your photo.

  • PNG, JPEG, or WebP, up to 10 MB, with the shorter edge at least 300 px. Export iPhone HEIC photos as JPEG first.
  • Write a prompt of up to 500 characters, or tap an idea chip to add one.
  • Length is 4–10 whole seconds (5 by default).
  • The clip keeps the photo's shape: a portrait photo makes a portrait clip, and a landscape photo makes a landscape clip.
  • Generate sound is on by default and doesn't change the price.

The estimate is a ceiling, so the real charge is usually lower. In Make videos, the References card has a Have a photo? Animate it → link that opens this mode.

MODE 2 · TEXT TO VIDEO

Describe a shot

No image needed. Describe the shot and the model makes it from scratch.

  • Choose a ratio: 9:16 (the default), 16:9, or 1:1.
  • Length is 4–10 seconds.
  • It costs more per second than the image modes, because the model starts from nothing.

This makes one short shot with no narration or captions. To turn a script into a narrated video, use Make videos.

MODE 3 · AI TRANSITION

Move from one image to another

Give a start image and an end image. The clip opens on the first and lands on the second.

  • The two images must be different and the same shape (aspect ratios within 2%). If they aren't, the form tells you before you submit. Otherwise the model would silently crop your end image.
  • Swap start and end flips them.
  • The prompt is optional. Left empty, it uses a smooth cinematic transition.
  • Length is 4–8 seconds.
MODE 4 · VIDEO → TALK/ACTION

Make your subject talk or act

Film your cat, then make it answer back. Upload a video, mark up to three moments on a timeline, and the app cuts to an animated version of that frame, then back to your footage.

  • Choose an MP4, MOV, WebM, or MKV, then click Upload. Choosing the file doesn't upload it. Once it's uploaded, its name, length, size, and frame rate show beside the button, and the editor opens.
  • The stage on the left shows the frame under the playhead. Click Play (or press Space) to watch your video with sound. The playhead follows it.
  • Scrub the timeline to the moment you want, then click + Add at playhead. Each moment is a span, up to 3 per job. The Spans list on the right shows each span, its cut time, and what it costs.
  • Click a span to edit it in the panel on the right. To move its cut, drag its diamond on the timeline, click the ruler, or type a time in Cut at (s).
  • The timeline has four tracks: Source (your video's frames), Animation (one block per span), Voice (each talk line's length), and Captions (click to turn a line's captions on or off).
  • Spans can't overlap. If a span doesn't fit, it turns red and a red line under the timeline says what to fix.

Phone videos work as filmed. A video shot sideways comes out upright. An HDR video is converted to standard range, so on an HDR screen its colours and highlights look flatter than the original, and the editor tells you so.

MODE 4a · A TALK SPAN

Give it a line

A talk span lip-syncs your subject to a line. Its block on the timeline is as long as the line, so you change its length by changing the line.

  • TTS voice: choose a voice, set its speed (0.25–4), and type up to 200 characters. Below speed 1 the limit is shorter, so a slowed line still fits. A Delivery box, when it shows, takes up to 300 characters on how to say it: tone, accent, pacing.
  • Your recording: choose an audio file up to 12 seconds. Its length shows once it uploads.
  • Captions: one of the caption styles, or none. They show only while it speaks.
  • If your app's talk model can take one, a How does it move while it talks? box appears, with idea chips. Leave it empty and the subject stays mostly still.

Change how a recording sounds. Under a recording, the Voice group has:

  • Pitch: up or down by up to 12 semitones, without changing speed.
  • Tone: −3 (darker) to +3 (brighter).
  • Echo: off, room, or hall.
  • Clean up: cuts rumble and hiss.

Click Preview to hear the changed voice. It's free. Reset puts every control back.

The model hears the changed voice, so the mouth matches it. Changing a control after a video exists means that span is generated, and paid for, again. None of these controls change the line's length.

MODE 4b · AN ACTION SPAN

Describe what it does

An action span animates the frame from a prompt. It's silent.

  • Describe the action in up to 500 characters. The motion chips add a subject move (turns to the camera, walks left, jumps onto the couch, sits down) or a camera move (holds still, slow push-in).
  • Length is 4–10 whole seconds. Type it, or drag the span's right edge on the timeline.
  • Seed: leave it empty and the model picks one each time, or click New seed. Re-run a take you like on the same seed to keep it.
  • Quality applies to the whole job. Draft runs action spans at low resolution for about a quarter of the price, so you can try ideas cheaply. Talk spans have no draft.

With Skip ahead to… chosen (next section), an action span can End on the real frame where your footage picks up again, so the cut back is seamless. A draft can miss that frame; Final lands on it.

MODE 4c · WHAT HAPPENS AFTER, AND THE SEAMS

Join it back to your footage

Every span has a Then setting for what your footage does after the animation:

  • Resume where it cut adds the animation, so the video gets longer.
  • Skip ahead to… replaces a stretch of your footage instead. Set where it picks up by typing a time or dragging the span's right diamond. The skipped footage shows striped on the timeline.

Seam blend softens the cut between your footage and the AI clip:

  • Blend in works at the cut and Blend out at the point where your footage resumes. Each dissolves over 0.1–2 seconds.
  • Click the curve to add a point, drag a point to shape it, and click a point to remove it.
  • A blend changes only the picture. It doesn't change what the animal does, and the sound still cuts where it did.
  • Trying a new curve on a span you've already made is free: open the job in the editor, change the curve, and click Animate.

The panel tells you when a seam has little to do, for example when there's no footage on that side or the clip already ends on the real frame.

MODE 4d · HEAR THE LINES FIRST

Check the words before you pay

Animating is the expensive part. Hear the lines first, beside Animate, makes only the voices and caption timing (pennies), then waits.

  • The job card shows prepared and a player for each talk line.
  • Like it? Click Generate on the card to animate it.
  • Want different words? Click Open in editor, change the line, and hear it again. Each take is its own card. Only the take you click Generate on gets animated and paid for. The others just stay parked and cost nothing.
  • A wording you've already heard is free to hear again.

The button needs at least one talk span. A job with only action spans has no lines to hear.

EVERY MODE · THE RESULT

Wait, then download

Each job gets a card under the form. A generated clip usually takes two to three minutes, and a talk/action job takes longer with more segments.

  • The three generated modes go Queued → Generating → Finishing.
  • A Video → talk/action card shows its status in the corner (queued, preparing, prepared if you chose to hear the lines first, generating, splicing, then done or failed) and one line per span for its audio, words, and animated parts, each ticked ✓ when ready. Action spans have no audio or words, so those two tick right away. Each line also lists the span's seed, end frame, and blends, so you know what to keep.
  • You can leave the page. If the app restarts, the job picks up where it stopped. A clip that was already generated isn't paid for again.
  • When it's done, the card shows a player and a Download link, and the editor's stage plays the finished video. Animate clips aren't part of blind picks or the Schedule queue, so download one and post it yourself.
  • To tune a talk/action job, click Open in editor on its card. The spans come back as you sent them. Change what you like and click Animate again. Spans you didn't change aren't paid for again.
  • Submitting the exact same job again costs nothing, because every paid call is cached.

If you asked for sound but the model sent back none, the card says the clip is silent.

VIDEO → TALK/ACTION · ON YOUR PHONE

Make one from your phone

The phone app runs Video → talk/action on an iPhone. Sign in with the same account as the web app. It's for admins, and only when Animate is turned on; otherwise a screen tells you which of those is missing.

  • Your videos lists the videos you've picked on this phone and the jobs made from them, each with its status. Tap New video to pick one from Photos. The editor opens right away while the video uploads in the background.
  • Scroll the filmstrip under the playhead to the moment, then tap Talk or Move to add a span there (up to 3). Talk and Move sit on separate lanes, in the order they play.
  • Talk: type a line or record one (up to about 12 seconds), choose captions, and set what happens after. Once a recording has sent, the Voice section has pitch, tone, echo, and clean up. Tap Hear it to hear the changed voice, free; the recorder's own play button still plays your raw take, so you can compare.
  • Move: describe what happens, set a length of 4–10 seconds, and optionally a seed or blend.
  • Tap Animate to review: one row per span, Final or a quick draft, the price, and anything still missing. Send it, or tap Hear the lines first.
  • On the job screen, a finished video plays full screen with Save to Photos, Share, Edit again, and New take. New take re-runs only the Move spans on a fresh seed, so the talk spans aren't paid for again.

Pick a video once and use Edit again from then on. Picking the same video again can make Photos hand over a re-encoded copy, and the app would treat it as new footage and pay for every span again. The price is hidden for a recording made in an earlier session, because the app no longer knows its length.

If something is greyed out or fails

  • Image, Text, and Transition are greyed out in the Mode menu. The AI video service isn't connected to your app yet. Hover a mode to see why, and ask whoever runs your app. Video → talk/action shows the same reason as a message.
  • TTS voices or captions are unavailable on a talk span. They need the app's OpenAI connection. Use Your recording with captions off instead, or ask whoever runs your app.
  • Animate is greyed out. Something isn't finished: a file not yet uploaded, a missing prompt or line, images that don't match, or spans that overlap or don't fit. The form says what in red, and a talk/action span that needs fixing turns red on the timeline.
  • The editor doesn't appear. Click Upload after choosing your video.
  • + Add at playhead is greyed out. The job already has 3 spans, or your spans leave no free spot in the video. If the playhead is inside a span, the new one goes to the nearest free spot instead.
  • Hear the lines first is greyed out. The job has no talk span, so there's nothing to hear.
  • There's no Voice group under a recording, or it says the server can't change a voice. Voice changes aren't set up on your app. Click Reset if the controls are set, and the job can go without them.
  • There's no "How does it move while it talks?" box. Your app's talk model doesn't take one, so a talking subject mostly stays still. Use an action span for bigger moves.
  • A recording says its length isn't known. The span came from an earlier job, so the timeline can't draw it and the price leaves it out. Choose the file again to measure it.
  • A job failed. The card shows the reason. Fix it if it names your input, then submit the same job again. Anything already generated is reused, so it isn't paid for twice.
